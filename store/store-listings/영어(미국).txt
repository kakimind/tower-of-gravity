A match-3 puzzle with a twist: gravity itself flips against you. Welcome to Tower of Gravity.

Swipe ingredients to match 3 or more — but every few moves, gravity reverses and the entire board comes crashing down in the opposite direction. Just when the board feels stable, everything shifts again.

◆ KEY FEATURES

🔄 Gravity Flip
Every few moves, gravity reverses and ingredients tumble the other way. No board ever stays settled for long.

💎 4 Special Ingredients
Row Clear, Column Clear, Cross Bomb, and Color Bomb. Buy them in the shop with Stardust, or find them through events as you climb.

🌟 Stardust Economy
Clear a stage to earn Stardust. Spend it on extra moves or special ingredients to help you through tougher climbs.

❤️ Heart System
Each run costs one heart. Hearts regenerate over time, and there's always a way to top up if you're in a hurry.

◆ MEET YOUR GUIDES

Five companions join you on the way up — the Old Archivist, the Will-o'-wisp, the Tower Gatekeeper, the Dust Sprite, and a fellow Apprentice Mage — each with their own voice and story as you pass through their floors.

◆ 1000 STAGES, 100 FLOORS

Low floors ease you in. High floors turn up the pressure — gravity flips more often, ingredients grow more varied, and every board gets harder to read.

Start climbing. See how high you can go.
